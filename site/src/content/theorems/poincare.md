## The problem

A vector field on the sphere lays a little arrow flat against the surface at
every point — a way of combing the hair on a coconut. Poincaré proved in 1885
that you can't comb it smoothly without leaving a bald spot: some arrow has to
shrink to nothing.

$$
v(x) = 0.
$$

Which means that somewhere on Earth, right now, the wind isn't blowing.
