## The problem

Flatten a sphere onto a plane however you like, without tearing it. Some pair
of opposite points always lands in the same spot:

$$
f(x) = f(-x).
$$

Right now there are two places on opposite sides of the Earth with exactly the
same temperature *and* exactly the same air pressure.
