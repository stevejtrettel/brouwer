## The problem

Take a filled-in disk and move its points around however you like, so long as
you don't tear anything. Some point always ends up exactly where it started:

$$
f(x) = x.
$$

Crumple a map of the world and drop it on a flat copy of the same map. However
you crumple it, some point of the crumpled map lies directly above its twin on
the flat one.
